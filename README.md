# 23 November 2026: the world in one image

One PNG (1024×1024) summarising the world news of Monday 23 November 2026 — a single poster carrying the day's six defining stories, from a sunken Russian corvette in Libya to the cost of getting home for Thanksgiving.

## The six stories on the poster

1. **Libya** — Ukrainian sea drones sink the Russian corvette *Merkury* at a Libyan port.
2. **Poland** — three Russian cruise missiles cross into Poland, which calls an emergency NATO session.
3. **Antalya** — COP31 ends without a deal.
4. **Santa Clara** — Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as chief executive.
5. **Washington** — Representative Jimmy Gomez resigns after the House Ethics Committee's finding.
6. **United States** — the most expensive Thanksgiving travel season in years.
